RapidRescue
ℹ️ About RapidRescue
RapidRescue is a smart highway emergency response platform built to reduce fatalities caused by ambulance delays, poor communication, and misinformation during road accidents.

🚨 Instant Reporting

Users can report accidents immediately with details and photo proof.

🚑 Faster Response

Nearest ambulance can be assigned quickly with ETA tracking.

🗺️ Live Monitoring

Track accident zones and ambulance movement on live maps.

📰 Verified Alerts

Official news reduces fake rumors and confusion.

📞 Emergency Help

All important support contacts available instantly.

⛑ Public Safety

First aid guidance before rescue reaches the location.

Future Scope: AI accident detection, drone support, smart traffic clearance, hospital auto-notification, and predictive danger alerts.

⛑️ First Aid Guide
Quick help before ambulance arrival.

🩸 Heavy Bleeding

  • Use clean cloth
  • Apply pressure
  • Raise injured part
  • Call ambulance

🦴 Fracture

  • Do not move area
  • Give support
  • Keep calm
  • Wait for help

🔥 Burns

  • Cool with water
  • No ice
  • Cover gently
  • Get treatment

😵 Unconscious

  • Check breathing
  • Open airway
  • Lay sideways
  • Call emergency
⚠️ First aid is temporary support only. Seek professional treatment immediately.
